# Jobvision ATS — Applicant Tracking System

Last updated: 2026-11-16

## Confirmed project scope
- Jobvision product, within employment period November 2023–April 2025; exact project dates are not separately confirmed.
- The designer's scope was the ATS dashboard and mobile presentation, not the entire ATS product or all ATS workflows.

## Problem observed with HR teams
- HR relied on Excel and fragmented reports/information.
- Tasks and information were scattered.
- It was difficult to understand candidate statuses, overdue tasks and priorities quickly.
- The designer worked directly with HR staff, collected their feedback and iterated on the dashboard.

## Rejected direction
Separate pages for each report were explored but rejected because they would preserve fragmented navigation and force HR to move between views to reconstruct the operational picture.

## Design decision
An integrated dashboard brought together:
- Data visualisation (charts/graphs)
- Task tracking
- Reminders
- Status tags/labels
- Prioritisation
- Candidate status information in the first-view overview

## Observed outcomes
- Overdue tasks became easier to identify through clear labels.
- Candidate status became less confusing after missing information was added to the first-view overview.
- Prioritisation became clearer.
- HR feedback was positive.
- The user recalls analytics showing increased engagement.
- The user recalls a substantial improvement in HR task-management speed, but has no exact timing or analytics values.

## Evidence caveats
- Increased engagement is a qualitative recollection of analytics, not a retained numeric result.
- Faster task management is a user recollection, not a measured before/after time saving.
- Do not invent percentages, time savings, conversion rates or benchmark comparisons.
- The designer did not own the complete ATS product.
