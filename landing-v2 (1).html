  • Story → three films → one cut
    Tension

    A story is only a film once someone commits to a cut. Generators never commit.

    Rejected

    Prompt in, video out. A slot machine with a render button.

    Chosen

    Noise is the canvas. The engine proposes three films, cuts one, and every decision stays visible on the graph.

  • Pattern → action → owner

    Chosen

    Every insight ends in an action with an owner and a deadline.
