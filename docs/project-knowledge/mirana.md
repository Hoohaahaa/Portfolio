# Mirana — Executive Talent Platform

Last updated: 2026-11-16

## Confirmed project facts
- Jobvision product portfolio; within employment period November 2023–April 2025. Exact project dates are not separately confirmed.
- The designer personally designed all of the following candidate-side areas:
  - Talent profiles
  - Search and discovery
  - Contact flow
  - Mobile experience
- Design scope was candidate-side only.
- The designer did not design the employer panel or employer-side workflows.
- The user remembers substantial traffic arriving from Jobvision, but does not remember the number.

## Product context
Mirana is an executive talent/headhunting service associated with the Jobvision group. Public reporting described its launch in Mehr 1402 (October 2023) and its focus on executive/senior talent and discreet recruitment.

## Problem and design reasoning
Executive talent discovery differs from a conventional job-board flow. Candidate profile representation, discovery and the next contact step need to work as a coherent candidate-side experience.

## Outcome and caveats
The portfolio can describe the connected candidate-side experience across profiles, search, contact and mobile. Do not claim employer-side product design, exact traffic, conversion, placements or hiring outcomes. The user's recollection of high traffic is qualitative only.
