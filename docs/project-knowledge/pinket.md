# Pinket — FMCG Marketplace

Last updated: 2026-11-16

## Confirmed project facts
- Employment period January 2022–June 2023.
- Two-person design team.
- The designer was relatively junior at the time and did not track detailed product-performance metrics.
- Design scope covered almost all of the main areas the designer worked on:
  - Customer shopping journey
  - Store dashboard and operations
  - Shopper and product-selection experience
  - Product information
  - Reports and store data
- The user remembers approximately 300–1,000 monthly users during their time there. This is a retrospective estimate, not an analytics export.
- The designer was involved before the later pivot toward a platform for store data across Tehran. The later pivot and its outcomes are outside this designer's contribution.

## Product context and design challenge
Pinket was an FMCG/online grocery marketplace connecting customer shopping, store operations and shopper fulfilment. Each side had different information needs and actions, while belonging to one connected service.

## Design reasoning
The case study should show the multi-sided nature of the product: customer shopping, shopper execution and store-side operations/reporting. Avoid reducing the work to one app or one dashboard.

## Evidence and caveats
- 300–1,000 monthly users is approximate remembered product scale during the user's involvement.
- Do not call it active users unless the metric definition is verified.
- Do not attribute that scale to the designer's work.
- Do not claim the later Tehran store-data platform pivot or its success as the designer's achievement.
- Do not invent GMV, order volume, conversion, retention or revenue improvements.
