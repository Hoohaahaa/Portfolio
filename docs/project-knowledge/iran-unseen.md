# Iran Unseen — Travel Planning & Local Experiences

Last updated: 2026-11-16

## Confirmed origin
The project came from the designer's personal experience spending three months in Yerevan. Through conversations with people there, the designer noticed that travelers often had practical questions about local life that ordinary booking websites did not answer: payments, shopping, places to visit, local customs and how to find trustworthy local guidance.

The product intention is to add a human and local layer to travel planning, so travelers can become better informed before arriving or find someone they can feel comfortable experiencing a trip with.

## Implemented and usable capabilities
- User selects a destination.
- The platform shows distance and estimated trip cost.
- It suggests additional options and activities along the journey.
- It surfaces offers and suggestions around the selected destination.
- A traveler can submit a request to connect with a local.
- The local-side registration experience is designed and implemented so locals can register and receive offers.

## Current operational caveat
Local connection requests currently arrive by email to the project owner. The request path and local registration experience are implemented, but this must not be described as a scaled, automated marketplace or a fully operational two-sided matching network.

## Problem and product direction
Traditional booking platforms can help someone reserve a destination but often leave practical local questions unanswered. Iran Unseen connects destination discovery, trip estimation, contextual suggestions and local connection requests in one travel-planning experience.

## Outcome and evidence
The product is implemented and usable. There are no confirmed user, booking, conversion or revenue metrics yet. Present impact through implemented capabilities and the breadth of the traveler/local journeys, not adoption statistics.
