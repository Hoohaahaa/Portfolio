# Humaani — AI Creative Workspace

Last updated: 2026-11-16

## Confirmed facts
- Product work for Rastar.
- May–September 2025 (four months).
- Persian and English.
- Web and mobile.
- One shared design system.
- End-to-end product design scope included information architecture, navigation, cross-module flows, design system and responsive/bilingual experience.

## Problem and design reasoning
Distinct creative capabilities needed to feel like one coherent workspace. The work focused on product structure and relationships between modules, not only individual screens.

## Design decisions
- Establish information architecture and navigation hierarchy.
- Connect cross-module journeys.
- Create a shared design system and reusable patterns.
- Support Persian and English, including language direction and layout adaptation.
- Extend the product language to responsive mobile layouts.

## Evidence and caveats
The four-month period, two languages, two surfaces (web and mobile) and one shared design system are confirmed scope indicators. They are not performance metrics. No conversion, retention, speed or usability uplift is currently evidenced.
