# Pulse Check — Employee Experience Platform

Last updated: 2026-11-16

## Confirmed project facts
- Jobvision product, within the employment period November 2023–April 2025; exact project dates are not separately confirmed.
- Built from zero, shipped and used in production (as stated in the user's published case-study material).
- The designer led product design in a two-person design team; the second designer mainly contributed critique and feedback.
- Product capabilities documented in the case study include survey creation, six supported survey frameworks, measurable indices, heatmaps, priority mapping, insight cards with response evidence/raw comments, owners, actions, deadlines, an action tracker and re-measurement in a subsequent survey cycle.

## Core problem
A conventional dashboard full of metrics does not necessarily help HR understand what a finding means or what should happen next. The product aimed to connect feedback collection to interpretation, ownership, action and follow-up measurement.

## Rejected direction and design decision
- Avoid stopping at static reports or disconnected numbers.
- Structure the experience around the lifecycle: survey question → response signal → index/heatmap → priority → evidence-backed insight → owner/action/deadline → action tracking → re-measurement.
- Keep quantitative patterns and qualitative comments connected so an insight retains its context.

## Outcome and evidence
The product was shipped and used in production. The design contribution connected survey operations, quantitative reporting, qualitative feedback and follow-through.

## Caveats
- Values such as 45 responses, 45% participation, favorability 70/100, +3 points and eNPS 76 shown in UI screenshots are sample/demo values, not verified company outcomes.
- Do not claim survey completion, engagement, retention or employee-satisfaction uplift based on demo values.
- The six frameworks are a documented product capability, not a performance metric.
- Existing public case-study page mentions a 20-person team and an organization with 1,000+ employees; treat these as published case-study context, not as independently verified outcomes.
